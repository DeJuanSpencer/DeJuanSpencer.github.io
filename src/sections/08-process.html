How it works

Four steps. Plain language.

  1. 1

    We talk.

    You tell me about your business. I ask how customers find you now.

  2. 2

    I build.

    You see it taking shape as I go. Nothing hidden, no surprises.

    First draft in about a week.

  3. 3

    You approve.

    We go through it together. Changes are welcome.

  4. 4

    We launch.

    Your site goes live and starts working for you.
